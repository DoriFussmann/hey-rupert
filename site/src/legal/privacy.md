## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on your interaction with Rupert, we may collect the following categories of information.

### Information you provide

- **Contact information:** name, business email address, telephone number, company, job title, and other contact details.
- **Company and fundraising information:** company description, business model, sector, fundraising stage, fundraising objectives, target raise, geographic markets, investor criteria, and related business information.
- **Fundraising materials:** pitch decks, company profiles, financial projections, business plans, and other materials you choose to provide.
- **Communications:** emails, contact-form submissions, meeting details, feedback, questions, and other correspondence.
- **Service information:** engagement details, campaign preferences, approved investor targets, outreach instructions, and service-related records.

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information or confidential third-party information unless it is necessary for the engagement and you are authorized to provide it.

### Investor and professional contact information

In providing investor research and outreach services, Rupert may process professional information about investors and their representatives, including:

- Names, professional roles, business email addresses, and other business contact details.
- Investment firm, fund, sector, stage, geographic focus, and publicly available investment criteria.
- Information from public professional sources and other sources used for investor research.
- Outreach records, email correspondence, replies, follow-up history, and related engagement information.

This information helps Rupert assess investor fit and coordinate relevant outreach on behalf of clients.

### Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, certain information may be collected automatically, depending on the website configuration and your browser settings. This may include:

- IP address and general location derived from it.
- Browser, device, operating system, and technical identifiers.
- Pages visited, referring pages, time spent on pages, and interactions with website content.
- Diagnostic, security, and website performance information.

We use Google Analytics for website analytics. The precise information collected, cookie configuration, retention settings, and available privacy controls depend on the configuration of our website and analytics account.

We may use personal information to:

1. Respond to inquiries and meeting requests.
2. Assess whether Rupert's services are suitable for a prospective client.
3. Understand a client's company, fundraising objectives, and investor criteria.
4. Research, identify, evaluate, and organize potentially relevant investors.
5. Prepare personalized outreach and manage follow-up communications on a client's behalf.
6. Record investor responses, maintain campaign visibility, and report activity to clients.
7. Provide, administer, improve, and support our services and website.
8. Communicate about engagements, service changes, and relevant business matters.

10. Comply with applicable legal obligations and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We do not promise that investor research or outreach will result in a response, meeting, investment, or completed financing.
