## Page — How It Works (`/how-it-works`)

- Eyebrow: How It Works
- Heading: From cold list to warm introductions.
- Subhead: Five steps between you and the investors who should be funding your round.
- Steps:
  1. Rupert understands your startup. — It starts with a conversation — not a 50-slide deck. Rupert's AI intake asks the right questions about your stage, sector, geography, team, traction, and what makes your startup worth funding. In ten minutes, it builds a profile sharper than most pitch memos.
  2. Rupert finds your investors. — Your profile is matched against a database of 10,000+ VCs, family offices, and angel investors — not by name recognition, but by their actual investment criteria: stage, sector, geography, check size, portfolio patterns. You get a curated list of investors who fund companies exactly like yours.
  3. Rupert builds campaigns per investor type. — A seed-stage fintech VC and a family office looking at climate tech are different conversations. Rupert segments your investor list into personas and builds outreach campaigns tailored to each one — subject lines, opening hooks, proof points, and follow-ups designed for how each type thinks and decides.
  4. You get warm leads. — Rupert runs the email sequences — sends, tracks, follows up. You see every open, click, reply, and bounce in real time. When investors respond, Rupert hands you the warm leads and you take it from there. No guesswork, no ghosting into the void.
  5. The call is the beginning. — On your intro call, we walk you through how Rupert works: the AI intake, how we'd match your investors, how campaigns are built. You leave understanding exactly how we'd run your raise — and whether it's a fit. It's 30 minutes to learn the service, no deck required.
