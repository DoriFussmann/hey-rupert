### Frequently Asked Questions

- Is Rupert a fundraising agency? — Rupert is an expert-managed investor outreach service. Rupert researches investors, develops outreach and manages the campaign process, while the founder retains direct ownership of every investor relationship.
- Does Rupert take a percentage of the raise? — No. Rupert does not take a percentage of capital raised.
- Does Rupert guarantee funding or investor meetings? — No. No credible fundraising service can guarantee that an investor will engage or invest. Rupert's job is to improve the quality and execution of the outreach process.
- Who is Rupert best suited for? — Primarily founders of venture-scale companies preparing for or actively raising Pre-Seed through Series A rounds.
- Do I approve outreach before it is sent? — Yes. Messaging and campaign direction are reviewed with you before outreach begins.
- Who receives investor replies? — You do. Investor relationships remain yours.

## Page — How It Works (`/how-it-works/`)

- Page title: How Rupert Runs Investor Outreach | Rupert
- Meta description: See how Rupert works in three stages: discovery and strategy, campaign launch, and meetings and momentum — with the founder in control of every conversation.
- Eyebrow: How it works
- Heading: How Rupert runs investor outreach.
- Subhead: Three stages. Research, targeting, writing, follow-up and pipeline support — handled by an operator, with you remaining in control of every conversation.

### Step 1 — Discovery & Strategy

- Summary: Map the raise and build a precise investor universe before a single message is written.
- Photo alt: Two people in conversation across a table, a notebook open between them
- Understand the Raise — It starts with a direct conversation with Dori — the company, the story, the round, traction, and what has already been tried. The goal is to understand what makes the company fundable and where the outreach should concentrate, not to run a generic intake form.
- Build the Investor Universe — Rupert researches a tailor-made target list from a proprietary investor database — filtered by stage, sector, geography, check size, and genuine fit. Names are selected with human judgment, not sprayed across a directory and called a match.
- Develop the Strategy — Positioning, investor segments, messaging, and campaign approach are defined around the company and the raise. A seed-stage specialist and a family office are different conversations. The strategy treats them that way before any outreach is written.

### Step 2 — Campaign Launch

- Summary: Write the outreach, get your approval, then run the campaign with disciplined follow-up.
- Photo alt: A printed investor outreach list on a desk, a few names already marked
- Craft the Outreach — Messaging is written for your company and tailored to each investor audience — subject lines, proof points, and follow-ups designed for how that type of investor actually decides. This is not a generic sequence with the company name dropped in.
- Review & Approve — You review the strategy, the target list, and the messaging before anything is sent. Nothing goes out without founder approval. Rupert does the work; you keep control of how the company is presented.
- Launch & Follow Up — Once approved, Rupert runs the campaign and manages follow-up with consistency and discipline. The operational load — sequencing, timing, and keeping the process moving — sits with Rupert so you can stay on the company.

### Step 3 — Meetings & Momentum

- Summary: Turn replies into the right conversations and keep the process moving through the raise.
- Photo alt: Two people at a small table with papers, in a working conversation
- Qualify the Interest — Every response is visible to you. Rupert helps identify which investors are showing real interest and which conversations should be prioritized, so time goes to the meetings that actually matter.
- Prepare for Meetings — When a conversation is worth taking, the story is sharpened for that investor — likely questions, relevant proof points, and what they will care about given their mandate. You walk in prepared, not improvising from a generic deck.
- Manage the Process — Rupert stays involved as conversations develop: follow-ups, next steps, and keeping momentum through the raise. Investor relationships remain yours. Dori is available to help manage the process without sitting between you and the investor.
