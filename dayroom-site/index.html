Post-acute care, managed for health plans by AI.

After a hospital stay, members move through skilled nursing and home health, and plans lose sight of them. Stays run long, paperwork arrives late, and avoidable readmissions follow. It is one of the largest costs a Medicare Advantage plan carries, and one of the least managed.

What we're building

An AI agent that follows every member from discharge to home. It:

  • collects and reads the clinical documentation from facilities and agencies
  • tracks each stay against what the member's condition should need
  • flags stays running long and members at risk of readmission, with the evidence attached
  • hands the plan's care team a short list of who needs a call today

Where we are

We're at the start. We're building the first version and looking for health plans to design it with.
